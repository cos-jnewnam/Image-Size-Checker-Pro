Image Size Checker Pro — Compression & Diagnostic Playground

Test single-image lossless/high-fidelity compression and bulk ZIP export. Each sample carries a realistic original filename (e.g., hero-dashboard.png, product-feature.jpg) so you can verify that filenames, formats, and pixel dimensions are strictly preserved.

Exact 16:9 aspect ratio (320×180), lightweight payload, explicit width/height, and descriptive alt text.

2. Distorted Aspect Ratio (avatar-square.jpg)

Intrinsic 1280×720 photo encoded at quality 1.0 (~310 KB) in a 280×158 slot. Compresses cleanly to ~75 KB keeping 1280×720 dimensions.
